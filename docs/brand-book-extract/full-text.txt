BEST PRACTICES
When integrating your logo into diﬀerent materials, it's crucial to ensure it retains its 
proportions and size appropriately.
For optimum visibility and eﬀectiveness, adhere to these size guidelines:
●
The minimum width for the full logo should not be less than 1 inch (2.54 
centimeters).
●
In digital applications, ensure that the logo is at least 200 pixels in width for 
the best clarity.
●
When resizing the logo, always maintain its original aspect ratio to avoid 
distortion.
Keep in mind the context and viewing distance when determining the logo's size. 
Larger logos may be required for signage and printed materials, while smaller 
versions are suitable for digital and mobile applications.
Consistently applying these logo size standards across all materials and platforms 
will strengthen your brand's professionalism and recognition. Always refer to these 
guidelines to ensure your logo remains impactful and legible in any context.

BEST PRACTICES
To ensure brand consistency and preserve the integrity of your logo, please avoid the following common errors when working 
with your brand's visual identity:
1.
Modiﬁcation: Always use the approved, original logo ﬁles provided for reproduction. Do not alter or recreate the logo in 
any way. The entire selected lock-up should be used as a complete unit without any alterations to size, spacing, or scale.
2.
Proportions: Do not stretch or alter the proportions or scale of the elements within the logo. Maintain the original 
aspect ratio.
3.
Text: Do not alter the weight or case of the text in the logo. Keep the text exactly as provided.
4.
Arrangement: Do not change the positions or size relationship of the elements in the logo. Maintain the approved 
layout.
5.
Eﬀects: Do not add any visual eﬀects such as a drop shadow or glow to the logo.
6.
Background: Do not place the logo over a busy photograph or textured background that may obscure its visibility or 
readability.
7.
Color: Ensure that the logo's colors align with the brand's palette. Do not use clashing colors or those outside of the 
approved color scheme.
8.
Additional Restriction: Avoid placing the logo version with a green color palette on backgrounds that are predominantly 
white. This ensures optimal visibility and readability, as the green version may not contrast well on white backgrounds.
By adhering to these guidelines, you contribute to the maintenance of a consistent and robust brand identity.

TYPOGRAPHY
Our primary font is the cornerstone of our brand's textual identity, playing a 
pivotal role in conveying our message with clarity and consistency. It reﬂects the 
essence of our brand and should be used with care to maintain its impact and 
legibility across various applications.
Font Guidelines:
1.
Font Selection: Always use the approved primary font as provided for 
reproduction. Do not substitute it with other typefaces.
2.
Size Consistency: Maintain consistent font sizes for headings, 
subheadings, and body text across all materials and platforms.
3.
Color: Ensure that the font color aligns with the brand's color palette. Do 
not use colors that deviate from the approved scheme.
4.
Background: Choose font colors and backgrounds that oﬀer high contrast 
for readability. Avoid placing text on visually busy or clashing backgrounds.
5.
Hierarchy: Maintain a clear typographic hierarchy for diﬀerent content 
elements, emphasizing important information appropriately.
