# Kura website design decisions

## Merchants are the central product audience

The navigation, hero, opening CTAs, and capability sections prioritize merchants. Sponsorship is communicated as one source of demand entering the merchant payment network. The site does not explicitly tell visitors it is a secondary use case.

## What the homepage sells

Merchant outcomes (more ways to be paid, reach, payment visibility, wallet ownership), not infrastructure jargon. Technical rails stay off marketing pages.

Implied merchant value (do not label as “remittance”): Caribbean merchants can accept **digital dollars at POS**, including spend backed by family abroad—so cross-border support that already enters the community can pay the local till, not stop short of it.

## Product lifecycle language

- Existing / live: merchant QR/OTP acceptance, merchant digital wallet experience, sponsor-to-merchant purchase flows, in-network B2B and invoice tools.
- Limited/market specific: merchant onboarding, off-ramp options, organizational disbursements.
- Planned: global accounts and cards.

Use product/market copy approved by Kura before production release.

## Copy tone (Nov 2026 rewrite)

Confident, concrete, merchant-outcome voice. Short sentences. No crypto/blockchain language, invented metrics, licenses, partner logos, or launch guarantees. Channels (programs, families, B2B) are demand sources for the merchant network—not the center of the story. Merchant signup: https://app.kuratek.com/merchant — Sponsor signup: https://app.kuratek.com/sponsor

## Landing redesign (Nov 2026)

Homepage is product-led: white chrome, gradient logo on navy chip, desktop nav, proof strip (no fake metrics), interactive checkout→wallet demo, demand hierarchy (merchant core + feeders), fees/settlement/trust strip, sticky mobile CTA. In-network B2B is live; global accounts/cards stay planned. Families messaging: no app for recipients—SMS redeem at locations, instant, 24/7.
